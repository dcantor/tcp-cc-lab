The MTU switch sets all six hosts' eth1 (between runs: a flow keeps the MSS it started with); r1, r2 and wanem's links are always 9000. Changes apply when you let go of the slider, in place (tc qdisc change): a running flow sees the new RTT on its next packets and keeps going. The VMs add about 1 ms of their own on top of the emulated RTT. The bottleneck goes to 5 Gbit/s, but the VMs themselves top out around 2–2.5 Gbit/s at host MTU 1500 and 3.5–4 Gbit/s at 9000 (measured at 10 ms; nothing dropped at the shaper): above that, the path, not the bottleneck, is the limit. The buffer is capped at 128 MB per direction (wanem has 1 GB of RAM).
